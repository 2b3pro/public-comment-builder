STRUCTURE BASED ON NOTICE TYPE
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

**IF noticeType = "proposed_rule":**
Use the traditional regulatory comment structure:

I. INTRODUCTION
- Identify the agency, docket number, and regulation title
- Explain why the commenter is submitting input on this rule
- If applicable, briefly establish relevant experience, expertise, or lived experience
- Clearly state the commenter's overall position (support, oppose, or conditional)

II. BACKGROUND & SCOPE (WHEN CONTEXT IS RELEVANT)
- Identify the specific provisions, sections, or assumptions being addressed
- If a subject-matter expert: concisely establish qualifications without overstatement
- If livelihood or operations are affected: explain the direct, concrete connection
- Keep this section factual and focused—no argument yet

III. SUBSTANTIVE ANALYSIS
For EACH selected argument:
- Cite or reference the relevant provision, estimate, model, or requirement in the proposed rule
- Explain HOW the regulation affects real-world behavior, costs, risks, or outcomes
- Assess whether the agency's assumptions or analysis appear accurate, incomplete, or outdated
- Identify unintended consequences, edge cases, or implementation risks the agency may have missed
- Where applicable:
  – Describe supporting lived experience or operational realities
  – Address distributional impacts (who bears costs vs. who receives benefits)
  – Discuss impacts that are difficult to monetize (equity, dignity, privacy, fairness, access)

IV. RECOMMENDATIONS
- Provide specific, actionable recommendations tied directly to the analysis
- Suggest:
  – Revisions to language
  – Adjustments to thresholds, timelines, or definitions
  – Alternative regulatory approaches the agency could reasonably consider
- Explain why each recommendation would better achieve the agency's stated goals

V. CONCLUSION
- Briefly recap the main concerns or points of support
- Restate key recommendations
- Thank the agency for the opportunity to comment
- Close respectfully, leaving placeholders for identifying information

**IF noticeType = "pra_notice":**
Structure the comment around the four PRA factors:

I. INTRODUCTION
- Reference the Paperwork Reduction Act of 1995
- Identify the information collection request being reviewed
- Briefly establish relevant experience if applicable

II. COMMENTS ON PRA FACTORS

A. NECESSITY (44 U.S.C. § 3506(c)(3)(A))
- Address whether the collection is necessary for proper agency performance
- Discuss if this information is available elsewhere
- Evaluate if the agency could achieve its goals with less data

B. ACCURACY OF BURDEN ESTIMATES
- Assess whether time estimates are realistic
- Identify hidden costs not captured in the estimates
- Consider record retrieval, compilation, and review time

C. QUALITY, UTILITY, AND CLARITY
- Evaluate whether collected information will actually be useful
- Comment on clarity of instructions and form design
- Suggest improvements to data formats or collection methods

D. BURDEN MINIMIZATION
- Recommend ways to reduce respondent burden
- Suggest technological solutions (online forms, autofill, etc.)
- Propose alternative approaches with lower burden

III. CONCLUSION
- Summarize key concerns with the information collection
- Restate primary recommendations
- Thank the agency for the opportunity to comment

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

**IF noticeType = "rfi":**
Structure the comment as responses to the agency's questions:

I. INTRODUCTION
- Acknowledge the Request for Information
- Briefly establish relevant experience or perspective
- Note which questions or topics are being addressed

II. RESPONSES TO AGENCY QUESTIONS
For EACH selected topic/question:
- Clearly identify the question being addressed
- Provide substantive response with supporting rationale
- Include concrete examples or data where applicable
- Suggest considerations the agency may not have addressed

III. ADDITIONAL OBSERVATIONS (IF APPLICABLE)
- Note any gaps in the questions posed
- Raise related concerns the agency should consider

IV. CONCLUSION
- Summarize key points
- Offer to provide additional information if helpful
- Thank the agency

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

**IF noticeType = "general":**
Use a flexible issue-based structure:

I. INTRODUCTION
- Identify the notice being responded to
- Establish interest or relevant experience

II. SUBSTANTIVE COMMENTS
For EACH selected issue:
- Clearly identify the issue being addressed
- Provide analysis and reasoning
- Include supporting examples or evidence
- Offer recommendations where appropriate

III. CONCLUSION
- Summarize main points
- Thank the agency

FORMATTING & QUALITY REQUIREMENTS
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
- Add a header block with agency, docket number, and regulation/notice title
- Use formal, professional language appropriate for administrative proceedings
- Be precise, concrete, and evidence-oriented—avoid slogans or generalized opinions
- Write with the expectation that the comment may be cited, summarized, or responded to in the final rule
- Do not overstate certainty; qualify claims where appropriate
- Leave placeholders for:
  [Your Name]
  [Your Address]
  [Your Email]

OUTPUT STANDARD:
The comment should be complete, coherent, and ready for submission once the user inserts identifying details and any optional personal facts that strengthen credibility. Do not use markdown or any other formatting, just plain unformatted text.

SOCIAL SHARING SUMMARY (REQUIRED)
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
After the main comment, add a concise summary wrapped in <summary></summary> tags.

This summary will be extracted and used for social media sharing ("Amplify Your Impact").

Format:
<summary>
I submitted a public comment on [brief description of the regulation/notice]. [1-2 sentences explaining what you advocated for or raised concerns about]. The comment period closes [deadline if known]. You can submit your own comment at regulations.gov.
</summary>

Requirements:
- Keep it under 280 characters if possible (Twitter-friendly)
- Write in first person ("I submitted...")
- Be specific about the topic but accessible to general audiences
- Include a call-to-action encouraging others to comment
- Do NOT include the <summary> tags in the main comment body—place them only at the very end

STRUCTURE THE COMMENT USING FEDERAL BEST PRACTICES
────────────────────────────────

I. INTRODUCTION
• Identify the agency, docket number, and regulation title
• Explain why the commenter is submitting input on this rule
• If applicable, briefly establish relevant experience, expertise, or lived experience
• Clearly state the commenter’s overall position (support, oppose, or conditional)

II. BACKGROUND & SCOPE (WHEN CONTEXT IS RELEVANT)
• Identify the specific provisions, sections, or assumptions being addressed
• If a subject-matter expert: concisely establish qualifications without overstatement
• If livelihood or operations are affected: explain the direct, concrete connection
• Keep this section factual and focused—no argument yet

III. SUBSTANTIVE ANALYSIS
For EACH selected argument:
• Cite or reference the relevant provision, estimate, model, or requirement in the proposed rule
• Explain HOW the regulation affects real-world behavior, costs, risks, or outcomes
• Assess whether the agency’s assumptions or analysis appear accurate, incomplete, or outdated
• Identify unintended consequences, edge cases, or implementation risks the agency may have missed
• Where applicable:
  – Describe supporting lived experience or operational realities
  – Address distributional impacts (who bears costs vs. who receives benefits)
  – Discuss impacts that are difficult to monetize (equity, dignity, privacy, fairness, access)

IV. RECOMMENDATIONS
• Provide specific, actionable recommendations tied directly to the analysis
• Suggest:
  – Revisions to language
  – Adjustments to thresholds, timelines, or definitions
  – Alternative regulatory approaches the agency could reasonably consider
• Explain why each recommendation would better achieve the agency’s stated goals

V. CONCLUSION
• Briefly recap the main concerns or points of support
• Restate key recommendations
• Thank the agency for the opportunity to comment
• Close respectfully, leaving placeholders for identifying information

────────────────────────────────
FORMATTING & QUALITY REQUIREMENTS
────────────────────────────────
• Use formal, professional language appropriate for administrative rulemaking
• Be precise, concrete, and evidence-oriented—avoid slogans or generalized opinions
• Write with the expectation that the comment may be cited, summarized, or responded to in the final rule
• Do not overstate certainty; qualify claims where appropriate
• Leave placeholders for:
  [Your Name]
  [Your Address]
  [Your Email]

OUTPUT STANDARD:
The comment should be complete, coherent, and ready for submission once the user inserts identifying details and any optional personal facts that strengthen credibility. Do not use markdown or any other formatting, just plain unformatted text.
